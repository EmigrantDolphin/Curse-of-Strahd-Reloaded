# Long Rest
* Reik bent 1 hp*
* 8 hours of rest
* interrupted if u do 1h of walking, fight, cast.
* Can do light activity: read, talk, eat, stand watch (no longer 2 h)

* Regain half your max of hit dice

# Level up
* +1 hit dice
* hit points 5 or 1d8    +const mod
* class benefits

# Fog
įžengus į rūką, po 2d4 minučių ėjimo išnyra nepažįstamoje svalich miškų dalyje.
* Kiekvieną kart kai išnyra DC n * 5 Constitution saving throw. Kitaip gauna **exhaustion**

# Exhaustion
1	Disadvantage on ability checks
2	Speed halved
3	Disadvantage on attack rolls and saving throws
4	Hit point maximum halved
5	Speed reduced to 0
6	Death

Long rest + food and drink reduces exhaustion by 1
