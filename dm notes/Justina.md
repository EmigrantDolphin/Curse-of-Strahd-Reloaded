### Combat abilities

**Sneak attack** - Once per turn, you can deal an extra 2d6 damage to one creature you hit with an attack if you have advantage on the attack roll. The attack must use a finesse or a ranged weapon.
You don't need advantage on the attack roll if another enemy of the target is within 5 feet of it, that enemy isn't incapacitated, and you don't have disadvantage on the attack roll.
**Cunning Action** - You can take a bonus action on each of your turns in combat. This action can be used only to take the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.
**Thieves' Cant** - talk in thievy way to another thieves. Only thieves who understand this cant cant talk to each other end convey hidden meaning. (takes 4 times as long). Also understand secret signs and symbols to convey where is loot, where is danger, where is safe for you to stay, etc.
**Expertise** (Check which got selected) - At 1st level, choose two of your skill proficiencies, or one of your skill proficiencies and your proficiency with thieves' tools. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make that uses either of the chosen proficiencies.
At 6th level, you can choose two more of your proficiencies (in skills or with thieves' tools) to gain this benefit
