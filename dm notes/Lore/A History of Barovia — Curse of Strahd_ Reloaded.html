Barovia

A timeline of the valley’s history as told in Curse of Strahd: Reloaded — four thousand years of goddesses, vestiges, conquerors and the Mists.

BU counts years Before Unification; BC is the Barovian Calendar, which begins in 1 BC with King Barov I’s founding of Zarovia. Dates marked c. are worked out from the text’s relative timings (“two thousand years later”, “forty years after…”).

    The Rozana

    The four sister goddesses the First Folk brought into the valley, each ruling a season.

    The Weaver

    Spring · the spider

    Eldest sister. Birth, family and new growth; maker of the three life-giving gems.

    The Huntress

    Summer · the wolf

    Hunting, war and young passion. The wolfir ran in her Wild Hunt.

    The Seeker

    Autumn · the raven

    Foresight, harvest and the wisdom of age. The wereravens served her will.

    The Dreamer

    Winter · the butterfly

    Rest, hope and the grave. Given no shrine, she fell — and became the Devourer.
